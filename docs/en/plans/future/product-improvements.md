# Product improvements

[中文](../../../zh-cn/计划/未来/产品后续需求.md)

Select an outcome and the affected [module](../../modules/README.md) before scheduling. These are candidates from the former roadmap.

- **Library**. Explore per-track activation preferences, bounded summary views and user-triggered import/export and recoverable cleanup. Keep statistics in the tag plugin and consented listening reports in their [own optional service](optional-services.md).
- **Downloads and recipes**. Consider task prioritization and richer timeline editing after specifying the relevant order/draft and persistence semantics. [Current playback/download behavior](../../modules/library/history-and-downloads.md).
- **Source preferences**. Assess demand for per-source preload, page and retry settings. Preserve cancellation and request budgets. [Listing contract](../../modules/sources/listings.md).
- **Startup and bundle cost**. Measure launcher/first expansion, production userscript payload and costly lazy entries before changing them. For mobile, verify the actual entry/dependency output. [Runtime guides](../../modules/README.md).
- **Grid performance follow-ups**. O03–O05 depend on measured costs in sidebar layout, visible-row projections or resource remounting. Preserve card geometry, media/task lifetime and interaction if a targeted change is worth doing. [Current grid investigation](../../changes/in-progress/grid-performance.md).
